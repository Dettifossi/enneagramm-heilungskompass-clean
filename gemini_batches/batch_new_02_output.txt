=== PORTRAIT: Donata Hopfen ===
[SUBTITLE] Management consultant, digital consultant, b. 1976 in Hamburg – Animal equivalent: Meerkat
[H2] 1. The Meerkat
[H2] 2. Duty: Security Through Departure
[H2] 3. The Seven Wing: Speed as Home
[H2] 4. The Work: From McKinsey to the Bundesliga Storm
[H2] 5. Light and Shadow: When the Group Votes Out the Guardian
[H2] 6. The Meerkat That Realigns Itself
[P] The meerkat keeps watch for the group. It is vigilant, cooperative, and deeply oriented toward security through belonging. But not every meerkat keeps watch in the same way. Some climb quietly onto the highest rock and methodically scan the horizon that is the Five wing, the meerkat of analysis. Others run off, mobilize the group, bring movement to the territory before the threat is even visible that is the Seven wing. Donata Hopfen belongs to the second kind.
[P] She is a management consultant, digital strategist, former CEO of Bild and the Deutsche Fußball Liga (German Football League). A woman who stepped into large, sluggish systems wanting to get them moving. Not out of self-interest, but from the deeply rooted SO6 drive: I am here for the group. I handle what needs to be done. I clear the way. The meerkat of the SO6w7 does not run away it runs ahead.
[P] Claudio Naranjo called the social Type 6 duty security arises through serving, through belonging, through fulfilling a task for the collective. But the Seven wing changes how this duty is lived. Where Melanie Kreis (SO6w5) finds security through structure, analysis, and quietly fulfilling her role, the SO6w7 seeks security through movement. Moving forward feels safer than standing still. Taking on a task feels safer than waiting. The SO6w7 serves but it serves with speed.
[P] Hopfen built her career on this pattern: McKinsey the classic starting point for analysts and strategists. Then digital leadership roles in the media and technology sectors. In 2021, she became CEO of Bild, Germany's largest tabloid media outlet a house under transformational pressure. In 2022, she took over management of the Deutsche Fußball Liga (DFL), the governing body of the Bundesliga. Every one of these projects was a system in transition. And Hopfen was the one who said: I will take this on. We are getting this moving.
[P] That is the difference from the SO6w5: Melanie Kreis builds security by growing deep into a structure. Donata Hopfen builds security by renewing structures. Both serve the group. But one holds fast, while the other shapes.
[P] The Seven wing (w7) brings something to the social Six that the pure SO6 would not develop on its own: enthusiasm, willingness to take risks, and the conviction that setting out is better than staying put. Where the SO6 naturally leans toward caution and hedging, the Seven wing opens the door to vastness. The result is a person who seeks belonging and creates it through charisma and the energy of departure rather than through quietness.
[P] Hopfen is considered a thought leader in digital transformation. She speaks publicly, gets involved, takes positions. Her style is not that of Melanie Kreis, who convinces in the background through quality Hopfen is visible, articulate, present. That is the SO6w7 as a leadership type: Security is not gained by retreating into depth, but through outward connection, through moving people, through the feeling that the group stays in motion.
[P] Where the Five wing directs energy inward analysis, understanding, knowledge as a foundation the Seven wing directs it outward: networking, ideas, the next step. The meerkat of the SO6w7 does not keep watch alone. It organizes the whole group to look out together.
[P] Hopfen's most famous and turbulent station was her time as CEO of the DFL (Deutsche Fußball Liga, governing body of the Bundesliga). She took office in November 2022 with a clear mandate: lead the Bundesliga into the digital future and tap into new revenue sources. At the center was an investor deal with the private equity firm CVC Capital Partners, which was supposed to bring around two billion euros to the DFL.
[P] What followed was one of the most spectacular conflicts in German football: Fans protested massively against the entry of investors, games were interrupted by tennis balls and other objects on the pitch, clubs withdrew their support. In February 2023 after barely three months in office Hopfen stepped down. The deal did not materialise.
[P] It was a classic SO6 experience in its harsh form: Hopfen had acted on behalf of the group, and the group turned against her. Not because the strategy was wrong, but because it tackled a spot where the fear and identity of football collided. The Seven wing had made her fast and decisive. The SO6 vulnerability hit her when belonging broke away.
[P] The light of the SO6w7 is its ability to set a community in motion. Hopfen is in demand as a consultant and speaker because she combines clarity, courage, and strategic vision. She can read systems, name opportunities, and carry people along. That is the SO6w7 on the bright side: It does not just serve it inspires.
[P] The shadow of the SO6w7 lies in speed. Where Melanie Kreis (SO6w5) brakes with the Five wing, thoroughly checks, and only then acts, the SO6w7 can run off too early before the group is truly ready to come along. The Seven wing generates momentum and enthusiasm, but enthusiasm alone does not build a bridge between a vision and the fears of the people expected to follow that vision. The DFL episode shows this pattern in its purest form: The departure was real, the necessity was real but the group was not yet where the vision already was.
[P] For the SO6, this is the deepest wound: Being cast out from the community by the exact same community one stepped up for. Hopfen made this experience public. And did not disappear afterward. That is the difference between the SO6 shadow and SO6 maturity: The mature Six knows that belonging does not hinge on a single group.
[P] After her resignation at the DFL, Donata Hopfen did not go into hiding. She advises companies on digital strategy and leadership issues, gives lectures, and engages in networks. The meerkat of the SO6w7 does not retreat to the safest rock to watch the world quietly from there. It looks for the next group that needs a guard.
[P] Two meerkats, one task, two completely different paths: Melanie Kreis has been keeping the same watch for the same corporation for years, steadfast and precise. Donata Hopfen has traversed several territories, weathered several storms, and kept moving forward multiple times. Both live the duty of the social Six. But one embodies it as persistence, the other as the courage to set out. The meerkat of the SO6w5 keeps watch because it stays. The meerkat of the SO6w7 keeps watch because it keeps starting anew.

=== PORTRAIT: Frédéric Chopin ===
[SUBTITLE] Composer, pianist, 1810–1849 – Animal equivalent: Hedgehog
[H2] 1. The Hedgehog
[H2] 2. Trust: The One Connection
[H2] 3. The Four Wing: Homesickness as Composition
[H2] 4. The Work: Pianissimo as Language
[H2] 5. Light and Shadow: Quills and Exhaustion
[H2] 6. The Heart in Warsaw
[P] The hedgehog is the animal of the sexual Type 5 nocturnal, quiet, with quills out of instinct, not malice. Anyone who encounters it immediately notices: This is not an animal that shows itself. It rolls up. It waits. And only someone who is truly trusted gets to see what lies underneath.
[P] Frédéric Chopin lived thirty-nine years, and during this time he composed a body of work that to this day sounds like no other: as if someone were revealing themselves completely, but only at the piano. In salons, not in concert halls. For few, not for many. He gave barely thirty public concerts in his entire life. The rest of the time he worked, loved, and wrote music that revealed more of him than he could ever have put into words.
[P] Claudio Naranjo called the sexual Type 5 the countertype of the Five because sexual energy breaks through the avarice of the Five and searches for deep, intense connection. His key concept for this subtype: trust. The SX5 does not seek distance, but the one connection that carries everything. The quills are for the world not for the person who is truly trusted.
[P] Chopin's greatest love was George Sand, the writer whose real name was Aurore Dudevant. They met in 1836 in Paris. Two years later, in the winter of 1838/39, they traveled together to Majorca Chopin ill, the climate harsh, the accommodations sparse. He composed the Preludes Op. 28 there, one of his densest works. The isolation of winter, the closeness to George Sand, the proximity to death from tuberculosis all of this is in these miniatures.
[P] The relationship lasted nine years, from 1838 to 1847. It ended bitterly over a dispute involving parenting issues and her son Maurice, not love itself. After the separation, Chopin composed hardly anything more. As if the connection that had sustained him had disappeared along with her. The SX5 has only a few such connections. If one breaks, something essential breaks.
[P] The Four wing (w4) brings something crucial to the sexual Five: the pain of loss as an artistic drive. The pure SX5 might close up completely. The Four wing turns this closedness into art it transforms the unspoken into form, the pain into sound.
[P] Chopin left Poland in 1830, shortly before the November Uprising against Russian occupation failed. He never returned. Warsaw, the Vistula, the birch forests of his childhood all of that stayed behind him, and after the failure of the uprising, he knew there was no going back. This longing runs through his entire work: The Mazurkas ninety-eight in number are not folkloric costumes. They are an exile's attempt to hold fast to home in sound.
[P] The Four wing also explains his artistic uniqueness: Chopin wrote no symphonies, no operas, hardly any chamber music. He composed almost exclusively for solo piano. The piano was his intimate realm his hedgehog burrow. In this space, he opened himself completely. Outside: quills.
[P] Pianissimo in music the instruction to play as quietly as possible is far more than a volume indication. It is an attitude. It is the choice not to push, not to overwhelm, but to draw the listener into the silence. In pianissimo, tension arises not through volume, but through restraint. The unsaid, the subtle, the hesitation between two notes all of this becomes audible because the noise is absent. And anyone who truly listens notices: These quiet moments are the most intense. The most radical deceleration is simultaneously the most attentive.
[P] Chopin's body of work is small in volume and immense in impact. Around two hundred compositions almost all for solo piano. The Nocturnes: twenty-one night pieces, each describing a different kind of silence. The Études Op. 10 and Op. 25: technical exercise pieces that are at the same time masterpieces of emotional condensation. The Ballades: four works that combine narrative and passion in a way that redefined the genre.
[P] Franz Liszt, the brilliant performer and counterpole, played large halls Chopin disliked the big stage. He played in the salons of the Parisian bourgeoisie, at dusk, for perhaps thirty people. Robert Schumann (SX7w6) wrote about Chopin's Op. 2 the sentence: Hats off, gentlemen, a genius. The genius himself preferred to sit in the background.
[P] Claudio Naranjo shared an image in his seminars that he used for the sexual Five with a Four wing: A flute player tries to play his instrument but all the holes of the flute are plugged except one. Through this single open hole, all the love streams out at once. Nothing can stop it because it has no other way. That is how it is with Chopin: The love inherent in a person of this structure finds its way and because almost all other channels are closed, it comes through the piano with an intensity that catches some off guard to this day. Anyone who listens to Chopin and thinks that could only be a Type 4, so romantic, so full of longing misidentifies here the sexual Five with a Four wing: love that does not disperse, but concentrates.
[P] What remains is precision. Not a note too many, not an expression without reason. The SX5w4 wastes nothing it distills. What emerges from this distillate can still be heard two hundred years later in every concert hall in the world.
[P] The light of the SX5w4 is the capacity for artistic self-opening completely, unreservedly, but only within its own medium. Chopin revealed more in his music than most people do in a lifetime. This honesty is the gift of his combination: the countertype revealing itself but only where it is truly at home.
[P] The shadow: exhaustion. Avarice for the Five is not about money in the case of the SX5, but energy. Chopin was physically frail early on tuberculosis accompanied him from his twenties. But he also exhausted himself in another way: through the intensity of the one connection, through the uncompromising nature of his artistic standards, through the exile he never processed. After the separation from George Sand in 1847, he collapsed. The last two years of his life were marked by weakness, loneliness, and the admission that he could hardly compose anymore.
[P] He died on October 17, 1849, in Paris at thirty-nine years old. His last wish: that Mozart's Requiem be played at his funeral. A person who himself had never stopped asking what comes after silence.
[P] Chopin was buried in the Père Lachaise Cemetery in Paris where he still rests today. But his heart lies in Warsaw. His sister Ludwika smuggled it out of Paris in an urn, across borders that would have been hostile to the dead man as to the living. Today it rests in the Holy Cross Church in Warsaw, walled into a pillar.
[P] There is hardly a more fitting image for the SX5w4: The body remains in exile. The heart returns home. What he could never give back as a human being the return to Poland, touching the soil where he was a child he granted himself in death.
[P] And the music? That stays everywhere. That is perhaps the secret of the hedgehog: What it carries inside, it does not share lightly. But when it shares it, it lasts for centuries.

=== PORTRAIT: Gloria von Thurn und Taxis ===
[SUBTITLE] Princess, entrepreneur & patron of the arts, b. 1960 – Animal equivalent: Hippopotamus
[H2] 1. The Hippopotamus
[H2] 2. The Self-Preservation Two: Survival as the Primary Duty
[H2] 3. The One Wing: Morality as Backbone
[H2] 4. The Passion: Pride as a Protective Shell
[H2] 5. The Transformation: From Excess to Matriarch
[H2] 6. The Gift: Steadfastness as a Cultural Force
[P] The hippopotamus is the animal of the self-preservation Two, and hardly any personality in post-war German history embodies this animal as fully as Gloria von Thurn und Taxis. The hippopotamus lives in two worlds: In the water it is weightless, playful, surprisingly agile. On land it shows its true weight. What most do not know: The hippopotamus is the most dangerous large mammal in Africa. It seems harmless until it sees its territory threatened. Then it acts without hesitation, without apology, with a force that completely belies its sluggish appearance.
[P] In the 1980s, Gloria von Thurn und Taxis was known as the punk princess: mohawk, parties, excesses, a life followed with enthusiasm by the tabloid press. When her husband Johannes died in 1990, leaving the family with debt in the billions and a chaotic corporate structure, the hippopotamus stepped out of the water. What followed was not a metamorphosis it was a revelation. Who Gloria von Thurn und Taxis really was only showed itself at the moment when everything was on the line.
[P] The self-preservation Two (SE2) is in the Enneagram tradition the subtype that directs the energy of the Two most directly toward its own existence and that of its inner circle. Naranjo called this subtype Me First: not egocentrism, but survival intelligence. The SE2 asks first: What do I need to endure? What does what belongs to me need to survive? It is the most concrete, grounded, least romanticizing of the three Two variants.
[P] When Gloria von Thurn und Taxis became a widow at thirty in 1990, she took on a task that would have overwhelmed most experienced managers: restructuring one of the oldest aristocratic fortunes in Europe. She sold real estate, artwork, land and kept St. Emmeram Castle in Regensburg, the core of the family identity. No sentimentality, no shyness about tough decisions, no hedging. That is the SE2 in its clearest expression: It knows what is essential and protects it with a determination that often unsettles outsiders.
[P] What is remarkable is that Gloria von Thurn und Taxis never spoke the language of management consulting. She spoke the language of responsibility. For the children, for the heritage, for the people who depend on this heritage. That is the SE2 perspective: Survival is not a selfish project it is the foundation on which everything else stands.
[P] The One wing (w1) brings something to the self-preservation Two that the pure Two does not possess on its own: an inner set of rules that sustains independently of external recognition. The One knows a right and a wrong. It tolerates no half-measures. It judges and first of all, itself. Combined with the SE2, it creates someone who not only knows what they want, but also knows how it ought to be.
[P] In Gloria von Thurn und Taxis, this wing shows itself primarily in her deeply rooted religious convictions. After her husband's death, she turned to the Catholic faith with great intensity, which in her biography represents no mere refuge but an inner consequence. The One wing needs a reference point beyond pragmatic survival. It seeks order, principle, something that is true, regardless of whether it is currently convenient.
[P] That she takes clear often uncomfortable positions in public on marriage, family, and life issues is not populism. It is the SE2w1 in its natural posture: I say what I consider right. Whether that goes down well or not is a secondary question. The hippopotamus does not back down when it considers its standpoint to be correct.
[P] The passion of the Two is called pride, and in its deepest form it is not arrogance, but a protective strategy: the belief that through one's own giving, achieving, and carrying, one is indispensable. The Two who is proud of what she does for others never has to ask whether she would also be loved without this achievement. The pride of the SE2w1 carries a special quality: It connects with a consciousness of status, dignity, and duty.
[P] Gloria von Thurn und Taxis carries her inheritance not as a burden, but as a mandate. The Regensburg Castle Festival, which she turned into a cultural institution of rank; the art collection, which she carefully maintained; the public presence, which she never shied away from all of this is an expression of a pride that serves no vain self-dramatization, but rather the conviction: This heritage is worth preserving, and I am the one who preserves it.
[P] The shadow of this pride, as with any Two, lies in the question that remains silent beneath it: What am I when I have nothing left to give? The SE2w1 answers this with even more action, even more posture, even more clarity. The hippopotamus builds its territory larger so that it never stands empty.
[P] Gloria von Thurn und Taxis's 1980s are well documented: the mohawk, the costume parties, the headlines. It would be easy to see a contradiction between that and the woman she is today. From an Enneagram perspective, there is none. The SE2 lives in extremes: It is fully present in the moment, fully in enjoyment when that is possible and fully in protection, fully in responsibility when that is required. The shift from Princess TNT to the matriarch of the House of Thurn und Taxis is not a rupture. It is the same character in a different life situation.
[P] What changed is the water in which the hippopotamus swims. In the 1980s, the water was society, parties, the youth of the moment. After 1990, the water became the castle, the heritage, the family, the faith. The hippopotamus did not change. It remeasured its territory.
[P] What Gloria von Thurn und Taxis gave to the House of Thurn und Taxis and the city of Regensburg can be quantified and not quantified at the same time. Quantifiable is the restructuring of a crumbling heritage, the building of a functioning corporate structure, the establishment of a cultural festival of international renown. Unquantifiable is what lies behind it: the ability not to break under extreme pressure, but to grow.
[P] That is the gift of the SE2w1: steadfastness that does not come at the expense of others. A hippopotamus holding its territory and within it creating space for culture, for family, for that which reaches beyond a single person. Gloria von Thurn und Taxis has proven that nobility is not a fate one inherits but a task one accepts or abandons daily anew. She accepted. The hippopotamus stands.
