Closeness Without Merging

True closeness requires two whole people meeting. Not one who adapts. Not one who disappears. When we merge, we lose ourselves. We no longer know what we think on our own – and what we have adopted from the other. The relationship becomes smaller instead of larger. Closeness needs two selves. Two people who can say: This is me. That is you. And in between lies the space where genuine encounter is born.

Where are you losing yourself in a relationship right now? What would be your next step back to yourself?

— Detlef Rathmer
