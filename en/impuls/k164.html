What Creativity Has to Do with Courage

Creativity reveals something about us – and thereby makes us vulnerable. What if it isn't good enough? What if it gets rejected? This is the reason many people bury their creativity. Not for lack of talent, but out of fear of judgment. Yet unexpressed creativity seeks other outlets – and sometimes they are not good ones.

What would you like to express – through words, music, pictures, dance, craft – that you have held back so far? What would be the smallest step to do it today?

— Detlef Rathmer
