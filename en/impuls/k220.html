Transitions in Life

Transitions are the most difficult times: between the old and the new, when the old no longer applies and the new has not yet arrived. These liminal spaces are uncomfortable. You don't know who you are right now. You have lost the orientation you once had. Yet it is in these in-between spaces that most new things emerge. They are not a time of crisis, but a time of birth.

Are you currently in a transition – moving from something old to something new? What is no longer – and what is not yet?

— Detlef Rathmer
