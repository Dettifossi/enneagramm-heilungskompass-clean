What Meditation Achieves – and What It Doesn't

Meditation is not a path to thoughtlessness. It is a path to observing thoughts. Meditation doesn't make you calmer in the sense of having fewer thoughts. It makes you calmer in your reaction to thoughts. The Enneagram and meditation complement each other: The Enneagram shows the pattern. Meditation shows how it feels – from the inside.

What do you experience when you sit quietly and observe – even briefly? Which thoughts keep resurfacing? What does that say about your pattern?

— Detlef Rathmer
