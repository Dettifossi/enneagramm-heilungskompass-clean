The Triads: Head, Heart, Gut

The Enneagram divides the nine types into three triads: Head, Heart, and Gut. Head types process the world through analysis and planning. Heart types through emotion and connection. Gut types through instinct and action. No triad is better than another. Each has its strengths – and its blind spots. The goal is not to escape your own triad. It is to discover and integrate the other two within yourself.

Which of the three triads is least developed in you – Head, Heart, or Gut? And what could help you involve it more today?
