What Success Truly Means

The world has a clear definition of success: money, status, influence, recognition. This definition is loud and compelling. But it is not the only one. What does success mean to you – truly? Not for your parents, not for your environment, not for Instagram. For you. The Enneagram shows that every type has its own quiet benchmark for success. Finding it is one of the most important questions of life.

If no one were watching today – no comparison, no judgment – what would you define as success?

— Detlef Rathmer
