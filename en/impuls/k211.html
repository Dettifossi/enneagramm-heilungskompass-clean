What Sadness Opens

Sadness opens us up. It makes us permeable to what is truly there. Those who are never allowed to be sad lose touch with themselves. Those who can never cry lose something human. Allowing sadness does not mean drowning in it. It means letting it pass through – so that it can leave.

What are you carrying inside right now that is sad – and that you haven't truly felt yet? Can you let it be for a moment today?

— Detlef Rathmer
