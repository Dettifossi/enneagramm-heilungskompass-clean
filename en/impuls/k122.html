What Fear Really Is

Fear is information. Not a weakness. Not a malfunction. It says: Here is something that is important to me. Here is something I want to protect. Here is uncertainty that I have not yet resolved. The problem is not fear. The problem is when we obey it without asking what it is showing us. When it rules instead of informs.

What is making you afraid right now – and what does this fear show you about what matters to you?

— Detlef Rathmer
