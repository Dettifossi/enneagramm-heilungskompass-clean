The Art of Pausing

Pausing is not standing still. It is the pause in which you gather yourself before moving forward. Whoever never pauses lives on autopilot. Doing what they have always done – without asking if it is still right. Whether it still points in the direction they want to go. Pausing is the bravest act in a world that celebrates speed.

Will you pause today – briefly, consciously? What do you perceive when you stop doing for a moment – and start being?

— Detlef Rathmer
