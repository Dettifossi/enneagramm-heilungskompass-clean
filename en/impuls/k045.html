The Heritage of Generations

Sometimes we carry wounds that are not our own. Wounds our parents carried. And their parents. Unprocessed stories passed down from generation to generation. The good news: This pattern can be interrupted. In our generation. With us. Whoever heals themselves heals retroactively and prospectively. That is not arrogance. That is responsibility.

Which wound in your family runs through the generations? And what can you do so it goes no further?

— Detlef Rathmer
