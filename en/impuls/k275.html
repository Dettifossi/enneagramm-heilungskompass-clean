The Paradox of Freedom

Freedom is the greatest gift – and the heaviest burden. Whoever is free must choose. Whoever must choose carries responsibility. Whoever carries responsibility can fail. That is the price of freedom. And yet: without it, no genuine life is possible. The alternative – doing what others expect of us, always – is convenience. But not a life.

In which area of your life did you last truly use your freedom – to choose, to shape, to decide? And in which have you not yet?

— Detlef Rathmer
