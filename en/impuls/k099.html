Joy in the Little Things

Life's big moments are rare. Vacations, celebrations, milestones – they make up perhaps five percent of our time. Life consists of the rest. Of the everyday. Those who can only be happy in the big moments are happy rarely. Those who learn to appreciate the small things – a good conversation, a beautiful morning, a meal with people they like – live richer than they realize.

What was the sweetest small thing in recent days? Something you might have barely noticed. Stay with it for a moment.

— Detlef Rathmer
