What Lies Beyond the Pattern

The Enneagram shows the pattern. It describes how we react, what drives us, what protects us. Yet behind it – beyond the pattern – is something that defies description. A depth that no system can fully capture. Getting to know the pattern is important. Even more important is pausing occasionally to ask: Who am I when the pattern falls silent?

Are there moments in your life when you didn't experience yourself as a pattern – but simply as present, open, free? What kind of moments were those?

— Detlef Rathmer
