What Envy Reveals to Us

Envy is uncomfortable – and that is why it is so insightful. What you are envious of shows what you yourself want. What you reject for yourself. What you don't allow yourself to have. The Enneagram knows envy as the passion of Type 4. But in truth, everyone knows it. And everyone can learn from it: What do I desire – that I do not yet permit myself?

Who are you envious of right now – or were recently? What is this envy trying to tell you about your own unfulfilled desires?

— Detlef Rathmer
