What Conflict in Partnerships Means

Conflict in a relationship is not a sign that something is wrong. It is a sign that two different human beings are living together. The Enneagram shows: different types have different needs, different communication styles, different fears. No wonder friction arises. The question isn't: do we argue? The question is: how do we argue?

How do you argue in close relationships? What is your pattern – attack, withdrawal, avoidance? And what would a more constructive path look like?

— Detlef Rathmer
