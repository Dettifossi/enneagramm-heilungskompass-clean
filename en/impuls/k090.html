When the Path Is Not Linear

We imagine growth as a straight line: Today better than yesterday. Tomorrow better than today. It is not like that. Growth is a spiral. You return to the same themes – but on a different level. You fall back – and only then realize how far you have already come. Relapses are not a failure. They are part of the path. Sometimes they are the most important part.

Are you currently in a setback – or in a spiral? And what does this moment reveal to you about what you haven't yet processed?

— Detlef Rathmer
