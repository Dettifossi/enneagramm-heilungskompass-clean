What Gratitude Truly Changes

Gratitude is not a trick. It is an attitude that literally rewires the brain – research shows this. The Enneagram teaches us that every type has a different relationship with gratitude. For Type 4, it sometimes feels like a betrayal of their own longing. For Type 6, like naivety. For Type 7, like standing still. Yet gratitude does not mean denying what is difficult. It means not overlooking what is present – while looking at what is missing.

What is present today – despite everything? Name three things. They can be small. And then: stay with them for a moment.

— Detlef Rathmer
