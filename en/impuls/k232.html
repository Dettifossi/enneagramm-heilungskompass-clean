What Journaling Does

Writing is thinking. Not the recording of thoughts already formed – but thinking itself. Those who write about what they've experienced understand it differently. Those who put feelings into words gain perspective. Journaling isn't the right path for everyone. But for many, it is the most direct contact with themselves.

Could you write for three minutes today – about what is occupying your mind, what you feel, what you need? Not for others. Just for you.

— Detlef Rathmer
