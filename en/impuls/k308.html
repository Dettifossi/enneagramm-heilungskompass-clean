When You Don't Like Yourself

There are days when you don't like yourself. Your face in the mirror bothers you. Your reactions annoy you. Your thoughts startle you. That is human. Yet living with persistent self-dislike means living at war with yourself. This hostility exhausts you – and it is not solved through criticism. It is solved through understanding.

What do you dislike about yourself right now? And: could you understand why you are that way – without excusing it, but also without condemning it?

— Detlef Rathmer
