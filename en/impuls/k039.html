The Silence Between Two People

There is silence that is uncomfortable – because you don't know what the other person is thinking. And there is silence that holds – because you can simply be there together, without words. The quality of silence between two people reveals much about the depth of their connection. Anyone who always needs conversation topics might be afraid of what the silence reveals.

Can you be silent with the person closest to you – without experiencing it as distance? What does it feel like in that silence?

— Detlef Rathmer
