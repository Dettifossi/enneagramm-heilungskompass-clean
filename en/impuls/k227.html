The Art of Arriving

When we go to someone we love – after a long absence, after a hard day, after a long time – there is a moment of arrival. This moment is precious. It deserves our attention. Those who arrive without truly arriving miss it. Those who arrive and are truly present turn it into a genuine encounter.

Is there someone you will be arriving to today – physically or in conversation? How can you be truly present when you arrive?

— Detlef Rathmer
