When Care Becomes Control

It begins with love. I care. I want you to be well. So I ask. Plan. Organize. Take over. At some point, the other person realizes: They can no longer take care of themselves – because someone else has always already taken care of it. And the one who cares feels exhausted and unseen. Genuine care leaves space for the other person. It asks: What do you need? Not: I already know what you need.

Is there someone in your life to whom you give so much care that you leave no space for them? What would happen if you took a step back?

— Detlef Rathmer
