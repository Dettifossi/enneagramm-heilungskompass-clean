When Boundaries Are Communicated Lovingly

A boundary does not need to be harsh to be clear. You can say: "I care about you – and this doesn't work for me." You can explain without justifying. You can stand firm – without inflicting harm. This takes practice. And the belief that an honestly set boundary does not destroy a relationship – but often makes it resilient in the first place.

Is there a boundary you want to set right now – but you're afraid of how it will be received? Can you phrase it with warmth rather than harshness today?

— Detlef Rathmer
