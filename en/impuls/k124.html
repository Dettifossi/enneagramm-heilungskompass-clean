Walking Through the Fear

There are things in life you only get when you walk through the fear. Not around it. Not underneath it. Through it. It is uncomfortable. Sometimes painful. And on the other side, something waits that wasn't possible before: Freedom. Clarity. The knowledge: I can do this. Bypassing fear protects us in the short term. Over time, it leaves us smaller than we are.

Is there a fear right now that you are walking around? What if you ventured a small step through it today?

— Detlef Rathmer
