What Defines Good Leadership

Good leadership is not control. It is empowerment. It is helping others bring out the best in themselves – not extracting the best from them. The Enneagram shows how different types experience and exercise authority. Type 8 leads through strength. Type 2 through relationship. Type 1 through principles. All can lead well – and all have blind spots.

If you carry responsibility for others: do you empower them – or do you control them? What step today would mean greater empowerment?
