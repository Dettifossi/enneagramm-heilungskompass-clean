The Art of Arguing

Constructive arguing is a skill that can be learned. It has rules: no generalizations. No "always" and no "never." Talk about your own experience, not about the other person's misconduct. The Enneagram shows that every type falls into their pattern during an argument: one freezes, another escalates, a third disappears. Knowing these patterns is the first step – even in the middle of a conflict.

How do you behave in an argument – automatically? And what would be your more conscious alternative?

— Detlef Rathmer
