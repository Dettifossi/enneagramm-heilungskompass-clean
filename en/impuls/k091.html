The Gift of Failure

What if every failure were a gift – disguised as a defeat? Not in a sentimental sense, but practically: What does this failure show me that I wouldn't have learned any other way? The Enneagram itself arose from this question: What drives people – even when it harms them? What lies behind it? What would be possible if we understood that? Failure, looked at honestly, is the most candid feedback life can offer.

What hasn't gone as you imagined lately? And what does that reveal to you – about yourself, your assumptions, your next step?

— Detlef Rathmer
