What Connects Imagination and Reality

Imagination is not an escape from reality. It is the ability to see what is possible within reality. All great changes began as imagination – as that which was not yet present, but conceivable. Those lacking imagination see only what is. Those who only imagine see only what could be. The art lies in combining both.

What would be possible in your life that you haven't yet allowed as a possibility? Let your imagination speak for a moment today without immediately correcting it.

— Detlef Rathmer
