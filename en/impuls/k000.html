Do You Want to Be Right – or Happy?

There is a moment in almost every conversation where we stand at a crossroads: Do I assert my point of view right now – or do I let go? The Enneagram shows us that every type reacts differently at this crossroads. One fights with arguments. Another withdraws. A third gives in – but internally, the fire keeps burning. The more honest question is not: Who is right? But rather: What do I really want to take away from this encounter? Sometimes, letting go of your own point of view is not defeat. It is the most mature form of strength.

Is there a situation right now where you want to be right – but it will cost you more than it is worth?

— Detlef Rathmer
