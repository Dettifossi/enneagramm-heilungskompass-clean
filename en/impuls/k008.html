Awareness Is Not an Achievement

Anyone who knows the Enneagram quickly falls into a trap: they begin to observe and evaluate themselves. Am I aware enough today? Have I recognized my pattern? Would this have happened to me in the past? Yet awareness is not a project that can be completed. It is not a goal to be reached. Awareness is a light that sometimes burns brighter and sometimes softer. And the most important thing about this light: It does not judge. It simply shines.

Can you observe yourself just once today – without judging at the same time? Just look at what is there. Do not evaluate.

— Detlef Rathmer
