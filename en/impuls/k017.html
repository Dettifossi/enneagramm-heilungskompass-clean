The Power of the Pause

We live in a culture that celebrates speed. Those who accomplish a lot are considered valuable. Those who pause feel the need to explain themselves. Yet the Enneagram reminds us: All nine types suffer from overactivity in their own way. Not because they would be lazy if they paused – but because the pause brings stillness. And in the stillness, what we have been avoiding surfaces. A pause is not standing still. It is the prerequisite for all genuine movement.

When was the last time you truly paused – without a phone, without a task, without a goal? Could you allow yourself twenty minutes of that today?

— Detlef Rathmer
