The Courage to Be Unfinished

We like to present ourselves as finished. As someone who has figured it out. Who has done the work. Who no longer reacts the way they used to. Yet life keeps devising new tests. The Enneagram is not a final destination. It is a compass. And a compass only points north – it does not walk for us. Being unfinished is not a flaw. It is proof that we are still on the journey. And being on the journey is all that life asks of us.

Where are you still on the journey right now – while trying to pretend that you have arrived? What if you simply let that be today?

— Detlef Rathmer
