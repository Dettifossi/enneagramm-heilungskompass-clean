What Awe Means

Awe is the opposite of indifference. It is the state of being touched by something – the universe, life, another person, beauty. Those who know awe have not stopped wondering. Those who wonder are alive within. The Enneagram itself deserves a kind of awe: it shows how rich and complex humanity is. How many ways there are to be a human being.

When did you last feel awe – in the presence of something vast, beautiful, inexplicable? And how did that feel?

— Detlef Rathmer
