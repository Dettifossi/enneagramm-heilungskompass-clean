The Intelligence of Feelings

Feelings are not the opposite of reason. They are another form of information. An unease that cannot be explained. An enthusiasm that makes no rational sense. A hesitation we ignore – until we regret it. The Enneagram shows that every type has varying access to their feelings. Some have too much – and become overwhelmed. Some have too little – and make decisions in a blind spot. The question is not: Am I feeling? But rather: Am I listening to what I feel?

What feeling are you currently trying to bypass or explain away? What does it tell you if you truly listen?

— Detlef Rathmer
