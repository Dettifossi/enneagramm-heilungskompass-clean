What Begins Tomorrow

Every morning is a fresh start. Not perfect. Not guaranteed. But possible. What was heavy yesterday doesn't have to be heavy today. What failed yesterday can succeed today. This is not naive hope. It is a real possibility – one that every new day brings.

What will you begin anew tomorrow – even if it is small? And what do you resolve to let go of tonight – so there is room tomorrow?

— Detlef Rathmer
